# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales – Excel Dashboard Project

## 📊 Project Overview
The **Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based analytics project designed to analyze daily sales performance of a coffee shop. The dashboard provides insights into **revenue trends, product performance, customer purchasing behavior, store operations, and peak business hours** — helping business owners make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze **daily, weekly, and monthly sales performance**
- Identify **top-selling products and categories**
- Track **revenue, orders, and average spend trends**
- Understand **customer purchase patterns**
- Determine **peak sales hours and weekdays**
- Assist business planning and decision-making

---

## 🚀 Key Features
✔ Interactive Excel dashboard with slicers  
✔ KPI cards showing revenue & order performance  
✔ Time-series trends for sales & transactions  
✔ Category-wise and product-wise performance  
✔ Peak hour & weekday insights  
✔ Clean and easy-to-use layout
